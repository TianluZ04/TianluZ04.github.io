Research

🔭 I study how stress exposure and daily experiences jointly shape adolescents' biological (cortisol, inflammation) and mental health, using longitudinal and diary data.

🧪 My research centers on three questions:

  • How stress exposure history amplifies, sensitizes, or inoculates adolescents' concurrent response to similar stress (example)
  • How to capture variation in adolescents' adaptation and maladaptation to day-to-day stress (example)
  • How parenting and family dynamics help build adolescents' resilience to stress (papers)
